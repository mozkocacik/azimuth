# Footer

`band` fill in both themes. Four link columns, then the certification row (ISO 9001, IATF 16949 …), then the legal block: the full legal entity with registered office, register court and VAT ID, and the legal links (Imprint, Privacy notice, Terms and conditions of sale, Cookie settings, Whistleblower system).

The legal block is required by law in many countries. Do not remove it.

**Content:** link columns, certificates, legal entity data, legal links.
