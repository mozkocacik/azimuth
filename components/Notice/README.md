# Notice

Product change notifications (PCN) and discontinuation notices are formal documents. They show the number, the dates and the affected parts before any explanation.

1px `border-strong`, 3px top line in `warning` (or `brand` for information), square corners. Title (h4) with the notice number, a StatusTag, a row of dates and parts in `data`, one paragraph, and links to the PDF and the replacement.

**Content:** number, title, issue date, last time buy, last delivery, affected parts, replacement, reason, links.
