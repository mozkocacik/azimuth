# GlobalHeader

Two bars. The utility bar (34px, `band`) holds links for investors, press, careers and locations on the left, and the region and language selector and the customer login (MyAcme) on the right. The main bar (68px, `canvas`) holds the logo, five main sections, the search field and the theme toggle.

The open or current section has a 3px `brand` line under it. Below 1100px the main sections and the search field move into the menu; the utility links move into the menu too.

**Content:** utility links, region and language, login, main sections.
